# NV exact streaming service-rate campaign scope

## Objective

Determine how much of the measured-rate model ceiling is claimable by raising
the production-cold service rate of exact quantized projection bodies.  The
campaign starts from the installed endpoint and does not book modeled,
isolated, or profiled savings as token-wall recovery.

## Population

The first population is the rate-deficient Q4_K single-projection family used
by attention Q, K, and O.  The first discriminator packs independent output
rows as multiple warps in one CTA while preserving one warp per row, the
installed vector loads, per-lane ownership, accumulation order, epilogues, and
weight bytes.  This changes CTA issue granularity without changing numerical
work or assigning multiple warps to one row.

If this axis closes with complete information, continue to the Q6_K V and down
families only with a distinct causal hypothesis supported by their cold
counters.  Previously closed Q/O loop unrolling, Q6 down row packing, four
warps per row, and full-grid QKV producer compositions are controls, not new
candidates.

## Promotion funnel

1. Render/structure gate: exact grid coverage, one warp per row, no duplicate
   or missing weight ownership, and unchanged output contract.
2. Numerical gate: elementwise bit equality on nonzero deterministic inputs.
3. Production-cold gate: candidate must improve complete kernel duration and
   the counters must identify a rate mechanism at unchanged DRAM payload.
4. Population gate: enumerate every admitted and bypassed model site.
5. Device gate: exact token hash and improved role bucket/device union.
6. Wall gate: fresh-process control/candidate/control bracket, initially at
   least seven repetitions and confirmed at nine or more before promotion.

A cold or device win that fails the complete wall gate is a composition wall.
An opaque output, incomplete census, invalid control, or missing byte/rate
accounting is an information wall and must be repaired before closing the axis.

## Ledger rules

- Token throughput authority is unprofiled wall latency: `tok/s = 1e6 / us`.
- The measured-rate model is re-estimated after each accepted change.
- No cross-session subtraction is booked.
- Exact and numerical byte-reduction lanes remain separate.
- Stop a candidate at the first complete negative gate; continue the campaign
  to the next supported mechanism until the ranked population is adjudicated.
